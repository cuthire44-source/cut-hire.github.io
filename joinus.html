Common Questions

How do I get started as a client? +
Join our Discord server and open a ticket. Tell us about your project — what style you want, your deadline, and your budget. We'll match you with the right editor and help you request a free sample before you decide to continue.
How do I apply as a video editor? +
Join our Discord and open an editor application ticket. Share your portfolio — showreel, past work, or examples of the style you specialise in. Our team reviews every application and gets back to you within 48 hours.
What if I'm not happy with the first edit? +
You can request a free sample first. If the sample fits, you and the editor agree on the price, deadline, and full project details before continuing. If you don’t like that editor’s sample, we can bring in another editor for your project.
What editing styles do you cover? +
Our roster covers cinematic editing, gaming content, short-form reels (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts), music videos, documentary, and brand/corporate video. If you're not sure what you need, tell us your goal and we'll advise.
Why Discord? +
Discord gives us private, organised channels for every client project. It's fast, free, and lets both clients and editors communicate in real-time — no email chains, no delays. Every ticket is its own private thread.
