Common Questions

How do I join as an outsourcer? +
Join our Discord server and open an outsourcer ticket. Share what type of projects you can bring, the usual budget range, deadlines, and what kind of editors or designers you need.
How do I apply as a video editor/designer? +
Join our Discord and open an editor/designer application ticket. Share your portfolio — showreel, past work, thumbnails, designs, or examples of the style you specialise in. Our team reviews every application and gets back to you within 48 hours.
How are projects given to editors/designers? +
Outsourcers send the project details, references, deadline, and budget first. Then we match the project with an editor or designer who fits the style, availability, and skill needed for that work.
What should be clear before work starts? +
The brief, price, deadline, files, references, revision limits, and delivery format should be clear before anyone starts. This keeps the work fair for outsourcers, editors, and designers.
What editing/design styles do you cover? +
Our roster covers gaming videos, short-form reels, cinematic edits, long-form YouTube videos, thumbnails, motion graphics, social media designs, brand visuals, and other creative content depending on the project.
Why Discord? +
Discord keeps outsourcers, editors, and designers in one organised place. Outsourcers can post project details, editors/designers can ask questions, files and references stay in the same ticket, and the team can track deadlines, updates, revisions, and approvals without messy email chains.
